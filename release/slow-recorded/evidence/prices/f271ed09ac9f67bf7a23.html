Description

Word Power Made Easy is a bestselling, authorized Penguin edition designed to help readers improve their English quickly and effectively. Authored by the renowned grammarian Norman Lewis, this classic resource goes beyond merely expanding vocabulary—it teaches a method for broadening knowledge as an integral part of language mastery. Suitable for beginners and advanced learners alike, the book helps readers excel in exams such as GMAT, GRE, IELTS, LSAT, SAT, TOEFL, and CAT, and equips them with the vocabulary and language skills needed for interviews, academic pursuits, and career advancement. With just 15 minutes of daily practice, learners can become self-assured speakers, improve personality, and achieve success in communication. Written in a lively and accessible style, it provides clear instructions, grammar rules, pronunciation guidance, correct word usage, and fun exercises with progress-tracking systems. Revision exercises, quizzes, and puzzle activities reinforce learning, ensuring practical application of knowledge. Since its first publication in 1949, Word Power Made Easy has helped generations of students and professionals build confidence in speaking, reading, and writing English, making it an essential reference for anyone seeking to master the language.
